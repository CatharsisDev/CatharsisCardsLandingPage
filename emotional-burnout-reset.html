What burnout actually is

Burnout isn't just being tired — it's the state of running on empty for so long that rest alone doesn't refill you anymore. It often builds quietly, through invisible labor and constant performance, until even small tasks feel heavy. This workshop isn't about pushing through harder. It's about honestly naming how depleted you are, lightening the load that's actually draining you, and rebuilding your capacity in a way that lasts.
