2. What information we collect

When you browse the site

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site — pages viewed, approximate location (derived from IP address, not stored precisely), device and browser type, and referral source. This relies on cookies and similar technologies. We also track when a guide's "Buy" button is clicked (which guide, which platform), so we can see which products are performing, without this being tied to your payment details.

Some sections of the site display embedded posts from Instagram, X (Twitter), and Pinterest. These platforms may set their own cookies or collect data when their embedded content loads, governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

When you purchase a guide

Checkout is handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your payment card details. Stripe collects your email address and payment information to process the transaction — see Stripe's Privacy Policy.

After a successful purchase, your email address and order details (which guide, which platform, purchase date) are stored in our internal order-fulfillment system so that we can email you the PDF guide and your bonus access code, and so we can investigate and resolve any delivery issues you report to us.

When you subscribe to our newsletter

If you enter your email address into the newsletter signup form, we store that address with our email delivery provider in order to send you occasional updates about Catharsis Cards, new guides, and reflections. We use it for nothing else, and we do not sell or share it.

Alongside your address we also record how you arrived at the signup — for example the page you subscribed from, the site that referred you, or campaign parameters in the link you followed. This tells us which of our pages and channels people find useful; it is not used to build a profile of you or to track you across other websites.

Subscribing is entirely optional and separate from buying a guide — purchasing does not add you to the newsletter. Every newsletter we send includes an unsubscribe link, and you can withdraw your consent at any time either through that link or by emailing us. Once you unsubscribe, your address is removed from the mailing list.

3. Why we process this data

  • To fulfil your order — necessary to perform our contract with you (deliver the guide and bonus code you paid for).
  • To operate and improve the site — our legitimate interest in understanding usage and fixing problems.
  • To send you our newsletter — based on the consent you give by submitting the signup form, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Analytics cookies — where required by law, based on your consent.

4. Who we share data with

We use the following service providers, each of which processes data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Google (Analytics, Apps Script, Sheets, Drive, Gmail) — site analytics and automated order fulfillment/email delivery
  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Instagram, X, Pinterest — embedded content on the site, only when those sections are viewed

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. International data transfers

Some of the providers above (including Stripe and Google) may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep your data

Order records (email address, purchase details) are kept for as long as needed to fulfil your order, respond to support requests, and meet our legal and tax record-keeping obligations — under the Estonian Accounting Act, accounting source documents must be kept for 7 years from the end of the financial year they relate to. Newsletter subscriptions are kept only until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' default retention settings.

7. Your rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area or another jurisdiction with similar protections, you generally have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; request correction or deletion of it; restrict or object to certain processing; request a copy of your data in a portable format; and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at findyourcatharsis@gmail.com.

8. Cookies

The site uses essential cookies (e.g. to remember your color theme preference) and analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4). Embedded social posts may set additional third-party cookies when displayed.

9. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to findyourcatharsis@gmail.com.
